Find a meeting. Online, in person, happening right now, or any day this week. Then prove you were there, with verified attendance reports.

RecoverySky is a meeting finder for AA, NA, CMA, and Recovery Dharma. It tracks live online meetings you can join in one tap, and more than 55,000 in-person meetings you can walk into — searchable by fellowship, day, time of day, and distance from you.

FIND A MEETING ONLINE
• See AA, NA, CMA, and Recovery Dharma meetings that are live right now
• One tap from the listing into the meeting (opens in Zoom Workplace — free, just have it installed)
• Every meeting's full weekly schedule, so you always know when it's on
• Filter by fellowship, day, and time

We keep the listings honest: meeting details are kept current, and every live listing is verified. When you tap Join, you get a room with others — not a broken link or a wrong password.

FIND A MEETING NEAR YOU
• 55,000+ in-person meetings across AA, NA, CMA, and Recovery Dharma
• Sorted nearest first, with the distance on every row — pick your own search radius
• Browse any day of the week, or narrow to morning, afternoon, evening, or overnight
• Every venue has a detail page: address, one-tap directions in your maps app, published contacts, and the full weekly schedule
• Your location is read only while you're searching or logging in-person attendance — never at app start — and browsing coordinates are never stored or logged

ATTENDANCE YOU CAN PROVE
If someone needs proof that you're showing up — drug court, DFS, probation, parole, a sponsor, or a treatment team — RecoverySky documents both kinds of meetings:
• Online attendance records the real time you spent in the meeting
• In-person attendance is GPS-verified: tap "I'm Here" at the venue, the app confirms you're actually there, then times your visit
• Your attendance becomes a clean, digitally-verifiable PDF, emailed straight to whoever's asking or yourself

Unlimited reports come with RecoverySky Premium ($6.99/mo), which also unlocks meeting reminders, encrypted cloud backup, and sync across your devices — and keeps RecoverySky alive and growing.

YOUR RECOVERY, IN ONE PLACE
• Clean-time tracker and milestones
• Money-saved tracker
• The 90-in-90 challenge, with a certificate when you finish
• Meeting history and a simple picture of your progress

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your recovery data is stored encrypted on your device. We don't track you and we don't sell your data — the only thing we'll ever sell is the subscription that keeps the app running. That will never change. RecoverySky is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, and our platform is built and operated on HIPAA-compliant AWS infrastructure.

There's a meeting on right now, and another around the corner this week. Open the app and find it — we'll be here.

========================================================
DECISIONS & RATIONALE
========================================================

- VOICE: friendly-technical replaces warm. We now name mechanisms when they
  build trust (GPS verification, encryption at rest, foreground-only
  location, real meeting counts) — the supervision/court audience and
  privacy-conscious users both respond to specifics. The two warm-era
  guardrails that are about SAFETY, not tone, still apply:
  - Hugo (the verification bot) stays invisible. Outcome, not method.
    That also rules out monitoring-flavored phrasing like "continuously
    checked while they're on" — it implies a presence inside the room and
    spooks meeting hosts and attendees. Say listings are "verified" (state,
    not activity) and stop there.
  - Sky AI + Social are NOT advertised — built but hidden pending content.
- TITLE/NAME unchanged (30/30): already max-length with the top fellowship
  tokens. The ASO pivot to "meeting finder" lives in the Apple subtitle,
  Google short description, and keywords instead.
- "55,000+" is a real number: 55,617 active in-person meetings measured live
  across all sources (2026-08, see CLAUDE.md "In-Person segment" note).
  Re-verify the count is still ≥ 55,000 before each store submission; round
  DOWN to whatever the data supports.
- RD (Recovery Dharma) claims are live as of 2026-08-04 — RD is a selectable
  fellowship in Settings/onboarding and the Live / Search / In-Person
  filters (`EXPO_PUBLIC_FELLOWSHIPS="AA,NA,CMA,RD"` in .env + eas.json).
  Do not publish this listing against a build made before that config.
- LOCATION CLAIMS are worded to match actual behavior, and must stay that
  way: browsing coordinates are foreground-only, never stored/logged/in
  analytics — but the VERIFIED check-in fix IS saved to the attendance
  record (that's what makes the report verifiable) and syncs if Cloud
  Backup is on. So we say "browsing coordinates are never stored", never a
  blanket "your location never leaves your device." The copy also discloses
  BOTH reads up front ("while you're searching or logging in-person
  attendance") — keep that pairing; listing only the search read would make
  the claim incomplete.
- "GPS-verified" chosen over "location-verified" — it's the phrase the
  court/supervision audience actually searches, and it's accurate
  (usePresenceCheck verifies a GPS fix against the venue).
- "court" kept in keywords (high-intent supervision audience); "near,me"
  added for in-person search intent.
- "pinkcloud" demoted to the alternate keyword list — the general-finder
  tokens (near me, court, rd) now outrank competitor capture. Swap lists if
  Pink Cloud referral volume says otherwise.
- Data Safety / privacy framing unchanged from the warm listing: "we don't
  track you / don't sell your data", never "we collect nothing" (the app
  requires an account and offers cloud backup + location features, all
  disclosed on Google's Data Safety form).
- NO emoji or exotic Unicode in the Apple Description field — App Store
  Connect rejects them. Emoji is fine in Google Play's description and in
  "What's New" on both stores.
- This file coexists with STORE_LISTING.warm.txt (the warm-voice
  alternative). Whichever ships, keep the other for A/B reference.
