========================================================
DECISIONS & RATIONALE
========================================================

- VOICE: friendly-technical replaces warm. We now name mechanisms when they
  build trust (GPS verification, encryption at rest, foreground-only
  location, real meeting counts) — the supervision/court audience and
  privacy-conscious users both respond to specifics. The two warm-era
  guardrails that are about SAFETY, not tone, still apply:
  - Hugo (the verification bot) stays invisible. Outcome, not method.
    That also rules out monitoring-flavored phrasing like "continuously
    checked while they're on" — it implies a presence inside the room and
    spooks meeting hosts and attendees. Say listings are "verified" (state,
    not activity) and stop there.
  - Sky AI + Social are NOT advertised — built but hidden pending content.
- TITLE/NAME localized (28/30): the English title's "Meetings" becomes
  "Juntas", the everyday Mexican-Spanish word for 12-step meetings, giving
  the listing two meeting synonyms across indexed fields ("juntas" in the
  name, "reuniones" in subtitle/short description). The ASO pivot to
  "meeting finder" lives in the Apple subtitle, Google short description,
  and keywords, same as the English listing.
- "55,000+" is a real number: 55,617 active in-person meetings measured live
  across all sources (2026-08, see CLAUDE.md "In-Person segment" note).
  Re-verify the count is still ≥ 55,000 before each store submission; round
  DOWN to whatever the data supports. Numbers keep the US-style comma
  separator (55,000) — this is es-US, not es-ES.
- RD (Recovery Dharma) claims are live as of 2026-08-04 — RD is a selectable
  fellowship in Settings/onboarding and the Live / Search / In-Person
  filters (`EXPO_PUBLIC_FELLOWSHIPS="AA,NA,CMA,RD"` in .env + eas.json).
  Do not publish this listing against a build made before that config.
- LOCATION CLAIMS are worded to match actual behavior, and must stay that
  way: browsing coordinates are foreground-only, never stored/logged/in
  analytics — but the VERIFIED check-in fix IS saved to the attendance
  record (that's what makes the report verifiable) and syncs if Cloud
  Backup is on. So we say "las coordenadas de búsqueda nunca se guardan",
  never a blanket "your location never leaves your device." The copy also
  discloses BOTH reads up front ("mientras buscas o registras asistencia
  en persona") — keep that pairing; listing only the search read would
  make the claim incomplete.
- "verificada por GPS" chosen over a looser "verificada por ubicación" —
  same reasoning as the English "GPS-verified": it's the phrase the
  court/supervision audience actually searches, and it's accurate
  (usePresenceCheck verifies a GPS fix against the venue).
- "corte" kept in keywords (high-intent supervision audience); "cerca,mi"
  covers the "cerca de mi" in-person search intent. "cristal" added for
  the CMA audience (the common es-US word for meth). Keywords are
  unaccented deliberately — search normalizes accents, and unaccented is
  what users type.
- "pinkcloud" demoted to the alternate keyword list — the general-finder
  tokens now outrank competitor capture. Swap lists if Pink Cloud referral
  volume says otherwise.
- Data Safety / privacy framing unchanged from the warm listing: "no te
  rastreamos / no vendemos tus datos", never "we collect nothing" (the app
  requires an account and offers cloud backup + location features, all
  disclosed on Google's Data Safety form).
- NO emoji or exotic Unicode in the Apple Description field — App Store
  Connect rejects them. Emoji is fine in Google Play's description and in
  "What's New" on both stores. The «guillemets» in the description are
  plain Latin-1 punctuation and safe on both stores.
- ES-US LOCALIZATION NOTES:
  - Tú form throughout — standard for es-US app-store marketing copy.
  - In-app UI terms are quoted verbatim from the shipped `app/i18n/es.ts`:
    «Estoy aquí» (imHere), "Unirse" (joinMeeting), "En persona"
    (inPersonSegment). If those i18n strings ever change, this listing
    changes with them.
  - "confraternidad" for fellowship (the term NA/AA Spanish literature
    uses), "tiempo limpio" for clean time, "padrino o madrina" for
    sponsor, "Sigue viniendo" for "Keep coming back" — all established
    12-step Spanish, not invented translations.
  - "probatoria, libertad condicional" for probation/parole — the terms
    used in US Spanish-language court contexts.
  - Fellowship names stay as their English initials (AA, NA, CMA) and
    "Recovery Dharma" stays untranslated — that's how the fellowships
    brand themselves to Spanish speakers in the US.
- This file coexists with STORE_LISTING.warm.txt (the warm-voice English
  alternative) and the English STORE_LISTING.in-person source it was
  localized from. Whichever ships, keep the others for reference.
