  anyway (2 active devices, Sep 2026 bulk reports).
- Fields under test: SHORT DESCRIPTION and FULL DESCRIPTION only. Icon,

  differ too, a win can't be attributed to the copy.
- App title is NOT changed in the variant (stays "RecoverySky AA NA CMA
  Meetings"). Keeping it fixed means the experiment measures voice, not name.
- 1 variant, audience 50% (Play's maximum for this form).
- Target metric: "Unique user install clicks". CHANGED from the planned
  "Retained first-time installers": Play no longer offers it — the only
  choices are install clicks and open clicks.
- Minimum detectable effect 6% (the largest offered; default 2.5%) and
  confidence 90% (the lowest offered). Play's estimate even at these
  loosest settings: 8,173 install clicks, ~390 days. At the 2.5% default
  it was 47,071 clicks and 140+ days. Our listing sees ~1,000 visitors and
  ~500–600 install clicks a month at ~50% conversion (Jul–Sep 2026: 59%,
  54%, 50%).
- So this is a DIRECTIONAL test, by choice (2026-10-02): it costs nothing
  and only half the traffic sees B. Read Play's running numbers and
  intervals after ~2 months. A large effect in either direction will show;
  a small one never will, and then voice probably doesn't matter much.
  Bigger-effect assets (icon, screenshots, short description alone) are
  the better next test if this one stays flat.
- What a result means: B changes the short and full descriptions together,
  so a B win says "the warm package converts better," not which field did
  it. If B wins, a follow-up experiment can split short vs full.
- Experiment variants are NOT indexed for search — Google ranks off the

========================================================
DECISIONS & RATIONALE
========================================================

- THE HYPOTHESIS: the friendly-technical voice (A) won the 4.8.0 rewrite on
  judgment, not data. B tests whether the warm, addict-to-addict voice —
  "just listen", "you have to want it", "built by someone who needed it" —
  converts and retains first-time visitors better, particularly people
  arriving early in recovery rather than with a court requirement.
- SAME FACTS AS A, ON PURPOSE. Every feature A claims, B claims (130,000+
  meetings, in-person, RD, live online, one-tap join, weekly schedules, search,
  whole-week browse, distance/start sort, venue pages, location disclosure,
  favorites/ratings, trackers, 90-in-90 certificate, online + GPS-verified
  attendance, PDF reports, Premium price and perks, nonprofit, encryption,
  HIPAA-compliant AWS). If B carried fewer features the test would measure
  "fewer features" instead of "warmer voice." Don't add a claim to one arm
  without the other.
- ORDER DIFFERS FROM A, on purpose — it's part of the voice. A leads with
  attendance proof in the opening line; B leads with emotion ("Reaching
  out is the hard part. Finding the meeting shouldn't be."), then finding
  a meeting, then trust
  ("Meetings that actually open"), and saves supervision for later. The
  supervision section keeps its own heading so court-driven visitors who
  scan still find it.
- Hugo (the verification bot) stays invisible. The old warm copy said we
  "constantly check that live meetings are really live" — CHANGED
  2026-10-01 to "every live listing verified" (A's wording). "Constantly
  checks" is monitoring-flavored: it implies a presence inside the room and
  spooks meeting hosts and attendees. State, not activity.
- LOCATION CLAIM carries both reads, same as A: "while you're looking for a
  meeting or checking in at one". The verified check-in fix IS saved to the
  attendance record (that's what makes the report verifiable), so we say
  "we never store or log where you browse from" — never a blanket "your
  location never leaves your device."
- SEARCH CLAIM: "type a meeting name, a city, or a word you remember" —
  matches what the search box does (filters already-fetched results by
  name, city, venue, notes, etc.). Never "search every meeting."
- "THE WHOLE WEEK AT ONCE" sits in the in-person section only, same reason
  as A: the "Any" day is offered for in-person, not online.
- OPENING LINE — CHANGED 2026-10-01 from "There's a meeting happening
  right now — and someone in it is exactly where you are." That line
  presumed the reader's state, was live-only, and repeated the short
  description's "right now". The new line names the reader's real hurdle
  and sets up both "you have to want it" and "Meetings that actually
  open". It's what most visitors read before "more", so it carries the
  experiment more than any other line.
- "late night" replaces A's "overnight" — warmer, same time bucket.
- "GPS-verified" kept even in the warm voice: it's the phrase the
  supervision audience searches and the one a caseworker recognises. Warm
  doesn't mean vague where vagueness costs trust.
- SHORT DESCRIPTION: leads with the feeling ("A meeting is on right now.")
  and still names all four fellowships plus "online or near you", so a
  visitor scanning the short line learns the same scope as A's. No emoji —
  Google's metadata policy discourages them in the short description.
- No "no account / no setup" claims. The app requires an account, and
  joining needs the free Zoom Workplace app installed. Both stated plainly
  (Zoom here; the account is implied by Premium/sync and disclosed on the
  Data Safety form).
- "We don't track you / don't sell your data" — never "we collect nothing"
  (account, cloud backup, and location features are all disclosed on
  Google's Data Safety form).
- "130,000+ MEETINGS" — CHANGED 2026-10-01 from "55,000+ in-person". One
  headline number for online + in person together; we deliberately don't
  quote an in-person-only count any more. Counted on prod (recoverysky DB,
  `meetings` where status = 'ACTIVE' and not closed): 131,655 active weekly
  meetings (123,630 in person). One row = one weekly meeting time
  (a group meeting Mon/Wed/Fri counts 3), the same convention as Meeting
  Guide / TSML; distinct groups would be 73,255 / 70,863. The raw 169,027
  row count includes deleted, paused and closed rows — never quote it.
  Not de-duplicated across sources. Re-count before each submission and
  round DOWN; keep A (../STORE_LISTING.in-person.txt), B
  (this file), and ../STORE_LISTING.warm.txt identical.
- Sky AI, Social, and passwordless email sign-in are NOT advertised — the
  first two are hidden, the third is merged but not yet shipped to stores.
- Dropped "Find your pink cloud" as a slogan long ago — the top competitor
  is literally named "Pink Cloud."
