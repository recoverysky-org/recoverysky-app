# Replies: email change not showing

> For support: pick the variant matching the cause you found in [../problems/email-change-not-showing.md](../problems/email-change-not-showing.md). Each section is a ready-to-send email body.

## sign-out-and-in

Hi [first name],

Thanks for writing. Your email address is updated on our side. Your phone just hasn't refreshed yet and is still showing the old one.

To refresh it:

1. Open Settings and tap "Logout".
2. Sign in again the same way as before, with your password or a code sent to [the corrected address]. For a code, tap "Continue with Email", or "Use a different email" if the screen offers "Send code to" your old address, and type the new address. Don't use the "Send code to" button in that case: the code would go to the old address.

After that, your new address should show up. Your records stay on your phone throughout, so nothing is lost. If you use more than one phone, do the same on each.

Reply if the old address is still showing.

Warmly,
RecoverySky Support

## different-sign-in-method

Hi [first name],

Thanks for getting in touch. Each way of signing in (a password, an email code, Google, Apple) can have its own address. Signing in a new way with a different address doesn't change the address on your original account.

Two things to know:

1. If you want your earlier records, sign in the way you did first.
2. The address on a Google or Apple sign-in is controlled by Google or Apple, so we can't change it.

Reply with how you signed in originally and which address you'd like to use. We'll help you work out the best next step.

Warmly,
RecoverySky Support

## apple-hidden-address

Hi [first name],

Thanks for writing. When Settings shows "Hidden by Apple", that is expected and nothing is broken. You chose Apple's Hide My Email option, so Apple gives us a private forwarding address instead of your real one, and the app doesn't show it.

A few helpful notes:

1. Always tap "Continue with Apple" on the sign-in screen to get into this account, on every phone.
2. Typing your real address into "Continue with Email" starts a separate account, which won't have your records.

Reply if you'd like help with anything else.

Warmly,
RecoverySky Support
