# Replies: data missing after new phone

> For support: pick the variant matching the cause you found in [../problems/data-missing-after-new-phone.md](../problems/data-missing-after-new-phone.md). Each section is a ready-to-send email body.

## turn-on-backup-and-sign-in

Hi [first name],

Thanks for writing. Your records may be waiting in your Cloud Backup. The backup needs to be switched on for each phone.

1. Sign in with the same account as before.
2. Open Settings. If you don't see the "Cloud Backup" section, tap "Restore Purchases" first.
3. Turn on "Back up attendance".
4. Keep the app open and connected. The first pass can take a few minutes, and it finishes with "All backed up".

Reports come across a few at a time, so give them a little while. Reply if records still seem to be missing after that.

Warmly,
RecoverySky Support

## sign-in-the-original-way

Hi [first name],

Thank you for your message. Your records are very likely safe, but under the account you used before. This phone is signed in with a different one (a different method or address), so it looks empty.

Please try this:

1. Open Settings and tap "Log Out".
2. Sign in the way you did on your old phone: Google, Apple, or an email code with your usual address.

Backed-up records belong to the account that saved them, so signing in the same way matters. If you used Apple's Hide My Email, always tap "Continue with Apple" on the sign-in screen. Reply if you aren't sure which one you used.

Warmly,
RecoverySky Support

## data-was-only-on-old-phone

Hi [first name],

Thank you for writing, and I'm sorry. From what we can see, your records were only stored on your old phone, because Cloud Backup wasn't switched on there. That means we have no copy to give back.

What you can do:

1. If your old phone still works, don't erase it. Sign in on it, and turn on "Back up attendance" in Settings if it's offered. Wait for "All backed up", then sign in on the new phone with the same account.
2. If your data came from our older app, Settings has an Import option.

If the new phone says "Can't unlock your local data" (this can happen after a phone restore), tap "Reset local data" and confirm. It only clears what was already unreadable. Then sign in, and anything backed up returns once Cloud Backup is on.

I'm truly sorry we can't do more if the old phone is gone.

Warmly,
RecoverySky Support
