## create-an-account

Hi [first name],

Thanks for getting in touch. You're using RecoverySky without an account, which lets you find and join meetings. Clean time, attendance records, reports and reminders need a free account.

To make one:

1. On the Meetings screen, tap "Sign in" on the card under the filters.
2. Choose Google, Apple, or "Continue with Email". With email, you'll get a six-digit code, and entering it creates your account.

Reply if you'd like a hand.

RecoverySky Support
