========================================================
DECISIONS & RATIONALE
========================================================

- Dropped "Find your pink cloud" as the slogan — the top competitor is literally
  named "Pink Cloud." Kept "pinkcloud" as a KEYWORD to capture their searchers.
- Hugo (the verification bot) stays invisible. Promise the outcome, not the method.
- No "no account / no setup" claims. The app requires an account, and joining needs
  the free Zoom Workplace app installed. Both are stated plainly.
- "We don't track you / don't sell your data" — chosen over "we collect no
  information," because the app requires an account and offers cloud backup/sync,
  which Google's Data Safety form discloses. A blanket "we collect nothing" would
  contradict that form and risk rejection.
- Sky AI + Social feed are NOT advertised — built but hidden pending content.
- "court" kept in keyword lists (high-intent for the supervision audience).
- NO emoji or exotic Unicode in the Apple Description field — App Store Connect
  rejects them ("This field contains one or more invalid characters"). The 🌤️
  and the old "⸻" divider both tripped it. Emoji is fine in Google Play's
  description and in the "What's New" notes on both stores.
