"Grateful for" is your gratitude list for the day, at the top of the "Record My Day" panel on the My Day tab's main screen. Add as many good things as you like. My Day needs the Premium subscription, on iPhone and Android.

## How to add to your gratitude list

You're in My Day → "My Day", under "Grateful for" on the "Record My Day" panel. With nothing added yet, it asks "What's one good thing today?"

1. Type in the "Something I'm grateful for…" field.
2. Tap "Add".

The entry shows at once, under the ones you added before, oldest first. Add as many as you like.

## How to change or delete an entry

On "My Day", "Grateful for" only adds. To change or delete today's entries, use "Rate your day":

1. On the "Record My Day" panel, tap "Rate your day" (or "See your night", then "Edit").
2. Go to the last step, "Gratitude".
3. Under "Today's gratitude list", tap "Change" to edit an entry, then "Save" or "Cancel". Tap "Delete" to remove one.

"Use" on that list copies an entry into the night's own line, "One thing you're grateful for tonight". The list and the night's line are kept apart: changing one does not change the other.

## Good to know

**Premium only.** "Grateful for" is part of My Day, which needs the Premium subscription.

**Length.** An entry can be up to 500 characters. "Add" can't be tapped for a longer one, or while the field is empty.

**Offline.** An entry shows at once and sends when you're back online.

**Where your list lives.** On this phone, encrypted, and on The Daily Dash's server for your account. Settings → "Account" → "Delete User Data" deletes it from this phone; what was already sent stays on The Daily Dash's server.

## If something's wrong

- **"That one couldn't be saved."**: that entry was not accepted. Tap "OK" to close the message. Sending the same entry again won't help.
- **"That one didn't save. Try again."**: tap "Add" again. The text is put back in the field for you.
- **"Getting My Day ready…"**: the tab is still connecting your account. Wait a moment.
- **"My Day couldn't connect"**: check your connection and tap "Try again".

Contact support@recoverysky.app and mention what the "Grateful for" section says and when it started.

## Questions people ask

**Q:** How do I add something I'm grateful for?
**A:** In My Day, on "My Day", type under "Grateful for" in the "Record My Day" panel and tap "Add".

**Q:** How do I delete a gratitude entry?
**A:** Open "Rate your day", go to the "Gratitude" step, and tap "Delete" next to the entry under "Today's gratitude list".

**Q:** Why can't I tap Add?
**A:** The field is empty, or the entry is longer than 500 characters.

**Q:** Is my gratitude list the same as the line in Rate your day?
**A:** No. The list is "Grateful for" on "My Day". The line, "One thing you're grateful for tonight", belongs to the night. "Use" copies a list entry into the line.
