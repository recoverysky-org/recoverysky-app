"Rate your day" is a short evening review. You rate each part of your day from 1 to 5, note what you did, and end with something you're grateful for. "Past nights" shows the nights you rated. It is in the My Day tab, which needs the Premium subscription, on iPhone and Android.

## How to rate your day

You're in My Day → "Today", on the "Rate your day" card. Before you rate, it says "Waiting for you this evening."

1. Tap "Rate your day".
2. Each step is one part of your day and shows where you are, for example "2 of 6". Answer "How was this part of your day?" by tapping 1 to 5. "A five: …" and "A one: …" describe the two ends.
3. Once you rate it, "What you did" lists that part's actions to tap. Ideas may also appear under "Sky suggests"; tap one to keep it. "A note, if you like" takes a few words.
4. Tap "Next". It waits until you have given a rating.
5. The last step is "Gratitude": write "One thing you're grateful for tonight". "Today's gratitude list" shows what you added today: "Use" puts an entry in tonight's line, "Change" and "Delete" edit the list, and "Add to today's list" adds another.
6. Tap "Done". The card then says "Saved. Rest well."

"Need support now?" is on every step and opens Meetings → "Online", where live meetings are listed.

## What you'll see after you rate

You're in My Day, looking at "Your night" for a day you rated. Open it from the "See your night" button on the "Rate your day" card.

- Each part of your day with its rating, the actions you tapped and your note. A part dimmed with "No longer in your pillars" was rated before it left your list.
- "Grateful for: …" with tonight's line.
- "Worth some care tomorrow: …" naming the parts that asked for care.
- If tonight's ratings say things are hard: "You don't have to get through this alone" with "Get support now".

"Edit" and "Delete" show while the night can still be changed. "Delete" asks "Delete this night? Your ratings and line go with it." Tap "Delete", or "Keep it".

## Past nights

You're in My Day → "Today". Tap "Past nights" on the "Rate your day" card. You see a month, starting on Sunday. A mark shows each night you rated, and a different mark a night that asked for care. "Previous month" and "Next month" move between months. Tap a day to open it. A day you skipped says "You didn't rate this day."

## Good to know

**Premium only.** Rate your day is part of My Day, which needs the Premium subscription.

**Connection.** "Your pillars need a connection the first time." "Past nights need a connection." "Sky suggests" ideas show only online. Offline, a saved night says "Saved on this phone. It will send when you're online."

**The gratitude line and the list are separate.** Tonight's line belongs to the night. "Today's gratitude list" is the same list as "Grateful for" on "Today".

**Where your nights live.** On this phone, encrypted, and on The Daily Dash's server for your account. Settings → "Account" → "Delete User Data" deletes them from this phone; what was already sent stays on The Daily Dash's server.

## If something's wrong

- **"Your night didn't load. Check your connection and try again."** or **"Your nights didn't load. Check your connection and try again."**: tap "Try again".
- **"This night couldn't be sent as it was."**: tap "Send again", or "Discard". "Discard puts back the night the server has."
- **"This night waited too long to send, so it can't be saved now."**: tap "Discard".
- **"This didn't save on your phone. Try again."**: tap "Done" again.
- **"Getting My Day ready…"**: the tab is still connecting your account. Wait a moment.
- **"My Day couldn't connect"**: check your connection and tap "Try again".

Contact support@recoverysky.app and mention what the Rate your day screen says and the date of the night.

## Questions people ask

**Q:** How do I change last night's rating?
**A:** Open the night from "See your night" or "Past nights" and tap "Edit". If there is no "Edit", that night can no longer be changed.

**Q:** Can I delete a night?
**A:** Yes, while it can still be changed. Open it, tap "Delete", then "Delete" again. Its ratings and line go with it.

**Q:** Where do I see the days I rated before?
**A:** Tap "Past nights" on the "Rate your day" card.
