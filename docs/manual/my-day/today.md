"My Day" is a tab for your day in recovery: a quote, where to focus, a check-in, a gratitude list, your routine, and "Rate your day" in the evening. It needs the Premium subscription and works on iPhone and Android. My Day is made by The Daily Dash. What you enter is kept on this phone and on The Daily Dash's server for your account.

## How to open My Day

You need to be signed in, with the Premium subscription.

1. Tap "My Day" in the tab bar along the bottom.
2. The tab opens on "Today". While it loads, it says "Getting your day ready…".

The first time, or after you sign in again, the tab may say "Getting My Day ready…" for a moment while it connects your account. There is no second sign-in: My Day uses the account you are already signed in with.

The tab shows only when all of these are true: you are signed in, you have Premium, and you are not using guest mode. It does not show on the web.

## What you'll see on Today

You're in My Day → "Today". The date is at the top. Below it, cards in this order:

- **"You don't have to get through this alone"** shows first, only when last night's ratings say things are hard. "Get support now" opens Meetings → "Online", where live meetings are listed.
- **"Today's quote"** shows the day's quote and who said it, on days that have one. Tap "♡ Favourite" to keep it; it then reads "♥ Favourited". Tap it again to remove the favourite.
- **"Where to focus today"** lists the parts of your life worth some care today, each with how you rated it last night, for example "Last night: 2".
- **"Check in"**: answer a few questions about how you feel, any time.
- **"Grateful for"**: add the good things in your day.
- **"Your routine"**: tick off your routine, by part of the day.
- **"Rate your day"**: rate your day in the evening and look back at "Past nights".

Each of the last four has its own page in this manual.

## Good to know

**Premium only.** Without Premium there is no My Day tab. If Premium ends, the tab disappears and nothing is deleted. It comes back, with your data, when Premium is active again.

**Where your data lives.** On this phone, encrypted, and on The Daily Dash's server for your account. A change shows at once. If you are offline, it says "Saved on this phone. It will send when you're online."

**Signing out.** Signing out keeps My Day's data on this phone. When you sign back in with the same account, My Day opens on the same data.

**Delete User Data.** Settings → "Account" → "Delete User Data" also deletes My Day's data on this phone. What was already sent stays on The Daily Dash's server for your account. Nothing in the app deletes that copy.

**Language.** My Day's words are in English, whatever language you choose in Settings.

## If My Day won't open

You tapped "My Day" and see one of these instead of "Today":

- **"Getting My Day ready…"**: the tab is connecting your account. Wait a moment.
- **"My Day couldn't connect"**: My Day could not reach its server. Check your connection and tap "Try again". It also tries again by itself when your connection comes back and when you return to the app.
- **"Your day didn't load. Check your connection and try again."**: tap "Try again".
- **"My Day's local data couldn't be opened"**: My Day's storage on this phone could not be opened. "Reset My Day's local data" deletes only My Day's data on this phone, then asks "Reset My Day's local data?" with "Anything not sent yet is lost." Tap "OK!" to reset. Everything already sent is kept on the server.
- **"This account was deleted"**: if it says "It can still be restored for a while.", tap "Restore". If "Restore didn't work. Check your connection and try again." shows, check your connection and tap "Restore" again. If it says "The time to restore it has passed.", there is nothing to restore.

## If something's wrong

Still stuck on "Getting My Day ready…", or "My Day couldn't connect" keeps coming back after "Try again"? Check your connection and reopen the app.

Contact support@recoverysky.app and mention what the My Day tab shows and since when.

## Questions people ask

**Q:** I don't see My Day.
**A:** The tab shows only when you are signed in with the Premium subscription, and not in guest mode or on the web. Check that Settings → "Subscription" says "Premium". If it doesn't and you subscribed, tap "Restore Purchases" there.

**Q:** Who can see what I put in My Day?
**A:** It is kept on this phone, encrypted, and on The Daily Dash's server for your account.

**Q:** What happens to My Day if I sign out?
**A:** Its data stays on this phone. Sign back in with the same account and it is all there.

**Q:** Why is My Day in English when my app is in Spanish?
**A:** My Day's words are in English for every language setting.
