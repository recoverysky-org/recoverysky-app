"My Day" is a tab for your day in recovery: a quote, where to focus, your routine, and a "Record My Day" panel with your gratitude list and "Rate your day" in the evening. It needs the Premium subscription and works on iPhone and Android. My Day is made by The Daily Dash. What you enter is kept on this phone and on The Daily Dash's server for your account.

## How to open My Day

You need to be signed in, with the Premium subscription. Without Premium, tapping "My Day" shows "Premium Feature": "My Day is available with a Premium subscription. Try it free for 7 days." Tap "See Premium" to see the subscription offer right there; once you subscribe, My Day opens.

1. Tap "My Day" in the tab bar along the bottom.
2. The tab opens on its main screen, titled "My Day" with the date under it. While it loads, it says "Getting your day ready…".

The first time, or after you sign in again, the tab may say "Getting My Day ready…" for a moment while it connects your account. There is no second sign-in: My Day uses the account you are already signed in with.

The tab shows when you are signed in and not using guest mode, with or without Premium. It does not show on the web.

## What you'll see on My Day

You're in My Day → "My Day". The date is at the top. Below it, cards in this order:

- **"You don't have to get through this alone"** shows first, only when last night's check-in says things are hard. "Get support now" opens the "Support" screen: "Find a live meeting now", and the 988 line in the US and Canada.
- **"Today's quote"** shows the day's quote and who said it, on days that have one. Tap "♡ Favourite" to keep it; it then reads "♥ Favourited". Tap it again to remove the favourite.
- **"Where to focus today"** lists the parts of your life worth some care today, each with how you rated it last night, for example "Last night: 2".
- **"My Daily Routine"**: tick off your routine, by part of the day. "Edit ✎" in its heading opens the routine editor.
- **"Record My Day"**: one panel for the day's record. "Grateful for" is at its top, where you add the good things in your day. Under it, one button to a row: under "End of day review", "Rate your day" in the evening, or "See your night" once you have rated; and "Past nights".

Grateful for, your routine and Rate your day each have their own page in this manual.

## Good to know

**First visit.** The first time you open My Day, it opens on "Welcome to My Day", a page about what My Day is and why a routine helps. Tap "Get started" to go on to My Day. It shows once on this phone.

**Premium only.** Without Premium the tab is still there, but it offers "See Premium" instead of opening My Day. If Premium ends, My Day locks and nothing is deleted. It opens again, with your data, when Premium is active again.

**Free until a date.** An account can be given My Day free until a set date. The app says so once, with "My Day Is Free for You" and "Open My Day". While it lasts, a line under My Day reads "My Day is free until" the date, with "See Premium". In the last three days, "My Day's Free Time Ends Soon" shows once. After the date, tapping "My Day" shows "Your Free My Day Has Ended": everything you added is kept, and My Day opens again with it when you subscribe.

**Where your data lives.** On this phone, encrypted, and on The Daily Dash's server for your account. A change shows at once. If you are offline, it says "Saved on this device. It will send when you're online."

**Signing out.** Signing out keeps My Day's data on this phone. When you sign back in with the same account, My Day opens on the same data.

**Delete User Data.** Settings → "Account" → "Delete User Data" also deletes My Day's data on this phone. What was already sent stays on The Daily Dash's server for your account. Nothing in the app deletes that copy.

**Language.** My Day's words are in English, whatever language you choose in Settings.

## If My Day won't open

You tapped "My Day" and see one of these instead of "My Day":

- **"Getting My Day ready…"**: the tab is connecting your account. Wait a moment.
- **"My Day couldn't connect"**: My Day could not reach its server. Check your connection and tap "Try again". It also tries again by itself when your connection comes back and when you return to the app.
- **"Your day didn't load. Check your connection and try again."**: tap "Try again".
- **"My Day's local data couldn't be opened"**: My Day's storage on this phone could not be opened. Tap "Reset My Day's local data". It asks "Reset My Day's local data?" with "Anything not sent yet is lost." Tap "OK!" to reset; this deletes only My Day's data on this phone. Everything already sent is kept on the server.
- **"This account was deleted"**: if it says "It can still be restored for a while.", tap "Restore". If "Restore didn't work. Check your connection and try again." shows, check your connection and tap "Restore" again. If it says "The time to restore it has passed.", there is nothing to restore.

## If something's wrong

Still stuck on "Getting My Day ready…", or "My Day couldn't connect" keeps coming back after "Try again"? Check your connection and reopen the app.

Contact support@recoverysky.app and mention what the My Day tab shows and since when.

## Questions people ask

**Q:** I don't see My Day.
**A:** The tab shows when you are signed in, and not in guest mode or on the web. If it says "Premium Feature" when you tap it, My Day needs Premium: check that Settings → "Subscription" says "Premium". If it doesn't and you subscribed, tap "Restore Purchases" there.

**Q:** My Day says it's free until a date. What happens then?
**A:** On that date My Day locks, and nothing you added is deleted. Subscribe with "See Premium" and it opens again with your routine and entries. If you already pay for Premium, My Day keeps going and the app never shows the free date.

**Q:** Who can see what I put in My Day?
**A:** It is kept on this phone, encrypted, and on The Daily Dash's server for your account.

**Q:** What happens to My Day if I sign out?
**A:** Its data stays on this phone. Sign back in with the same account and it is all there.

**Q:** Why is My Day in English when my app is in Spanish?
**A:** My Day's words are in English for every language setting.
