"Check in" is a short set of questions about how you are feeling. You can check in as often as you like, from the "Record My Day" panel of the My Day tab, which needs the Premium subscription, on iPhone and Android.

## How to check in

You're in My Day → "My Day", on the "Record My Day" panel. Under "Check in throughout your day" it has a button for each check-in on offer, named for it.

1. Tap the check-in's button.
2. The first question asks how you feel, from 1 to 5. Tap one answer.
3. More questions follow, one at a time, one tap per answer. A question you type an answer to says "Optional". Tap "Next" to go on, or "Previous" to go back.
4. Tap "Done" on the last question. You see "Thank you for checking in."
5. Tap "Back to today".

Every question after the first has "Skip", except the one where you pick topics. While the next question loads, it says "One moment…".

## What you'll be asked

The questions depend on your first answer.

- **A low answer (1 or 2)** is followed by a question about what's bothering you, a question about self-harm, asked on every low day, then questions about the topics you pick.
- **A higher answer** is followed by a few questions about what you find hard. A question you left unanswered last time may come first.

**Support.** "Need support now?" is on every question. If your answers say things are hard, a card appears first: "You don't have to get through this alone" with "Get support now", or "Thank you for being honest" with "See support". Each one opens Meetings → "Online", where live meetings are listed.

## Leaving partway

You're in the middle of a check-in and tap "Close".

- Before your first answer, "Close" just goes back.
- After it, you see "Leave for now?" and "What you've answered is kept." Tap "That's enough for now" to finish with what you answered, or "Keep going" to go back to the question.

## Good to know

**Premium only.** Check in is part of My Day, which needs the Premium subscription.

**Offline.** "Your first check-in needs a connection. Check yours and try again." After that, the first question works offline. The questions after it need a connection. If they don't come in a few seconds, you see "Some questions need a connection." and can tap "Done".

**Saving.** Your answers show at once. If you are offline, it says "Saved on this device. It will send when you're online."

**Where your answers live.** On this phone, encrypted, and on The Daily Dash's server for your account. Settings → "Account" → "Delete User Data" deletes them from this phone; what was already sent stays on The Daily Dash's server.

## If something's wrong

- **"This check-in couldn't be sent as it is."**: tap "Send again", or "Discard" to drop it.
- **"This check-in waited too long to send, so it can't be saved now."**: tap "Discard".
- **"This didn't save on your device. Try again."**: answer again.
- **"This check-in isn't available."**: this check-in is not offered right now. Go back to "My Day".
- **"Getting My Day ready…"**: the tab is still connecting your account. Wait a moment.
- **"My Day couldn't connect"**: check your connection and tap "Try again".

Contact support@recoverysky.app and mention what the check-in screen says and which question you were on.

## Questions people ask

**Q:** How often can I check in?
**A:** As often as you like. Each time is a new check-in.

**Q:** Why does it ask about self-harm when I say I feel bad?
**A:** After a low answer (1 or 2), that question is asked on every low day.

**Q:** Can I stop halfway through?
**A:** Yes. Tap "Close", then "That's enough for now". What you've answered is kept.
