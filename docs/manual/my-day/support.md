"Support" is a screen for a hard moment: "Find a live meeting now" and, in the US and Canada, the "988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline". It opens from My Day, so it needs Premium, and it works on iPhone and Android, not on the web. RecoverySky does not show support contacts: Home has no "Support" card, and this screen lists no people.

## How to open Support

You're in the My Day tab. Support opens from two places there:
- **"Need support now?"**, a button under every step of "Rate your day". Open it from "Rate your day" on the "Record My Day" panel, in the evening.
- **"Get support now"**, on a card that says "You don't have to get through this alone". My Day shows it when your ratings say things are hard: first on My Day → "My Day", and on "Your night" after you rate your day.

Support has no way in outside My Day. The close button at the top right takes you back.

## What's on the Support screen

You're on the "Support" screen.
- **"Find a live meeting now"** opens Meetings → "Online", where you can join a meeting that is running right now.
- **"988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline"**, with a call button and a text button. This row shows only when your phone's region setting is the US or Canada. Tapping a button opens your phone's own calling or messaging app with 988 ready, so you press the phone's own button to place the call or send the text.

## Good to know

**Support contacts are not shown.** Earlier versions let you add your sponsor and other people to a "Support" card on Home and to this screen. RecoverySky no longer shows them anywhere, and it offers no way to add people. To call your sponsor, use your phone's own Contacts or Phone app.

**People you added earlier.** Hiding them deleted nothing: anyone you added stays in your phone's secure storage, never sent to RecoverySky's servers or anywhere else. Settings → "Account" → "Delete User Data" removes them, as does deleting the app and installing it again on the same phone.

## If something's wrong

- **I can't find Support**: it opens only from My Day: "Need support now?" under each step of "Rate your day", or "Get support now" on a hard day.
- **The 988 row is missing**: it shows only when your phone's region setting is the US or Canada.
- **"Couldn't open the phone app"**: your phone has no calling or messaging app that can take the number, as on some tablets. Dial 988 from another phone or app.

Contact support@recoverysky.app and mention that you're using the Support screen, what you tapped and which message you saw.

## Questions people ask

**Q:** How do I call my sponsor from the app?
**A:** RecoverySky doesn't show support contacts, so call your sponsor from your phone's own Contacts or Phone app.

**Q:** Where is the crisis line?
**A:** In the US and Canada, the "Support" screen has a "988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline" row with a call button and a text button. It opens from "Need support now?" in "Rate your day" or "Get support now" in My Day.

**Q:** Where did the Support card on Home go?
**A:** It was removed, along with support contacts on the Support screen. Anyone you added earlier is still stored on your phone and was not deleted, but the app doesn't show them.
