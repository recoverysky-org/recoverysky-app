"My Daily Routine" is the list of things you mean to do each day, grouped by part of the day: "Morning", "Afternoon", "Evening" and "Any time". You tick items off on "My Day" and change the list in the routine editor. It is in the My Day tab, which needs the Premium subscription, on iPhone and Android.

## How to tick off your routine

You're in My Day → "My Day", on the "My Daily Routine" card.

- Tap an item, its ring or its name, to mark it done. Tap it again to mark it not done.
- There is no Skip: an item is done or not yet. An item set aside earlier still reads "Set aside today"; tap it to mark it done.

Under each item, a line says "Done", "Set aside today" or "Not yet", and how many times you did it this week, for example "3 this week".

## How to start or change your routine

You're in My Day → "My Day". With no routine yet, the card says "Start with a routine someone has already thought about, or build your own." Tap "Start your routine". With a routine, tap "Edit ✎" in the card's heading (a screen reader reads it as "Edit routine").

The editor lists every part of the day, "Morning", "Afternoon", "Evening" and "Any time"; an empty one says "Nothing here yet". Each item has three small buttons on its line: "✎" (Edit), "⧉" (Copy) and "⏻" (Switch off), and a grip at its left.

- **"+"** at a part's heading adds an item there: type "What is it?", tick the "Parts of the day" it belongs to ("Tick more than one to add it to each."), and if you like a "Time (optional)", for example 07:30. Tap "Save".
- **"✎"** on an item: rename it, change its parts of the day, change or clear its time ("No time"), then "Save". Ticking another part adds a copy of the item there; unticking its own part while another is ticked moves it.
- **"⧉"** on an item adds a copy of it right after it, for you to rename or move.
- **"⏻"** on an item switches it off, as does "Switch off" inside "✎": "It leaves your routine. What you've done stays." Switched-off items are listed under "Switched off", each with two buttons: "⏻" (Switch on) brings it back, and "🗑" (Hide) takes it off the "Switched off" list for good. Hide asks nothing first, and what you did with the item stays.
- **Drag** an item by the grip at its left to change the order, within its part of the day or into another one.

With a screen reader, the buttons read "Edit …", "Copy …", "Switch off …", "Switch on …", "Hide …" and "Add to …" with the item's or the part's name, and each item offers the actions "Move … up" and "Move … down" in place of the drag.

## How to start from a template

You're in the routine editor, on "Start from a template". It is open by itself when your routine is empty; otherwise tap "Show templates".

1. Tap a template's name to see its items by part of the day.
2. Tap "Add these to my routine". It says how many items it adds, for example "Adds 3 items after your own."
3. "Other templates" goes back to the list.

Templates need a connection.

## Good to know

**Premium only.** My Daily Routine is part of My Day, which needs the Premium subscription.

**Saving.** Ticks and changes show at once. If a change could not be sent, it says "Saved on this device. It will send when you're online." Adding a template is the one change that needs a connection.

**A long list.** If you see "That's a full day. Would a shorter list feel lighter?", that is a gentle suggestion. Nothing is removed.

**Where your routine lives.** On this phone, encrypted, and on The Daily Dash's server for your account. Settings → "Account" → "Delete User Data" deletes it from this phone; what was already sent stays on The Daily Dash's server.

## If something's wrong

- **"Your routine didn't load. Check your connection and try again."**: tap "Try again".
- **"Templates need a connection. Check yours and try again."** or **"That didn't go through. Templates need a connection."**: connect, then try the template again.
- **"A change to your routine couldn't be sent."**: tap "Send again", or "Discard". "Discard also drops the routine changes made after it."
- **"A change to your routine waited too long to send, so it can't be saved now."**: tap "Discard".
- **"This didn't save on your device. Try again."**: make the change again.
- **"Use a time like 07:30"**: type the time as hours and minutes, or tap "No time".
- **"Getting My Day ready…"**: the tab is still connecting your account. Wait a moment.
- **"My Day couldn't connect"**: check your connection and tap "Try again".

Contact support@recoverysky.app and mention what the routine screen says and which item you were changing.

## Questions people ask

**Q:** How do I add something to my routine?
**A:** In My Day, tap "Edit ✎" on the "My Daily Routine" card (or "Start your routine"), then "+" at the part of the day you want. Name it and tap "Save".

**Q:** How do I take something off my routine without losing what I did?
**A:** Tap "⏻" on the item, or "Switch off" inside "✎". What you've done stays, and "⏻" under "Switched off" brings it back.

**Q:** Can I skip an item for today?
**A:** No, there is no Skip. An item is done or not yet, so leave it unticked. An item that reads "Set aside today" was set aside earlier; tap it to mark it done.

**Q:** How do I clear an item out of "Switched off"?
**A:** Tap "🗑" (Hide) on it. It leaves the list for good, and what you did with it stays.

**Q:** Can I use a ready-made routine?
**A:** Yes. In the routine editor, open "Start from a template", pick one and tap "Add these to my routine". Its items go after your own.
